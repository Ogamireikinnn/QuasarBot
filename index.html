Everything a partnership server needs

One bot for ads, roles, protection and the small things around them.

Partnership tracking

Every ad is saved. Ads with no valid partner mention are removed, with a short note on how to fix them.

Automatic Rep Role

Partners get your Rep Role right away. Delete the ad and the role is removed, unless another ad still counts.

Join-and-leave protection

Ban anyone who joins and leaves within 24 hours. Moderator kicks are left alone.

Trap channel

Pick a channel nobody should post in. Hacked accounts and spam bots that do get banned or kicked. Staff are exempt.

Welcome messages

Greet new members in a channel, in DMs, or both. With Membership Screening, it waits until they accept the rules.

Auto responders

Reply to trigger words in one channel or everywhere. Use variables like {user} and {server}.

Auto reactions

Add emojis to every new message in a channel, great for polls and suggestions.

Daily scan

Once a day the bot checks the ad channel again and fixes roles it missed. It follows your own timezone.

Full logs

Partnerships logged, ads removed, bans made. Everything goes to the log channel you choose.

Set up in three steps

You need the Manage Server permission to add a bot.

Add QuasarBot

Press Add to Discord, pick your server and approve the permissions.

Run /setup

Choose an ad channel, a Rep Role and a log channel. Put the bot's role above the Rep Role.

Press Save Changes

The bot tells you if a permission is missing. Run /config any time to check again.

Frequently asked questions

The Rep Role is not being given out

Usually QuasarBot's own role sits below the Rep Role. Open Server Settings, then Roles, and drag QuasarBot above it. Run /config and it lists anything else that is wrong.

Who can use the setup commands?

The server owner and anyone with the Manage Server permission. Staff with Manage Messages are never counted as ad partners, and they are exempt from the trap channel.

Do I have to use the bans?

No. Join-and-leave protection is a switch in /setup, the trap channel only works if you pick one, and the rep leave action can just delete ads without banning.

What happens if I remove the bot?

QuasarBot deletes your server's settings, tracked ads, responders, reactions and welcome message.

What data does it store?

Your server's settings and the ads it tracks. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

Something is broken. Where do I get help?

Run /report to open a private ticket, or ask in the support server.
