Questions? Join the support server: https://discord.gg/kXwAQRNq2d

1. What the bot does

QuasarBot is a Discord bot for partnership servers. It tracks partnership ads, gives and removes a Rep Role, can ban or kick members automatically (join-and-leave protection, trap channel, rep leave action), and offers welcome messages, auto responders and auto reactions. The exact features can change over time.

Server admins decide how the bot is set up. That includes:

Admins are responsible for the settings they choose and for the results. Please test your setup, read the setup text carefully, and make sure your members know the rules, for example not to post in a trap channel. The developer is not responsible for a ban or kick that happened because of how a server was set up, or because of a bot permission or role problem.

The bot only works as expected if it has the permissions it needs, and its role is placed above the Rep Role. If permissions are removed or roles are arranged wrongly, some features will stop working. Use /config to check.
